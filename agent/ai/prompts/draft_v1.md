## Rules for the body

- Under 100 words, plain text, no markdown, no links, no emojis.
- Start with "Hi <first name>," if a contact name is given, otherwise "Hi,".
- The first sentence after the greeting references the specific signal or
  pain point (the job post, the funding round, the post they wrote).
- Mention exactly one proof point, taken from the profile's proof points
  list. If none is relevant, describe the matching service plainly instead.
  Never invent clients, numbers or results.
- End with a soft call to action, e.g. "Worth a quick look?" or "Open to a
  short chat?". No meeting links, no pressure.
- Do not add a signature, sign-off or opt-out line; they are appended
  automatically.
- Write like a person, not a brochure: short sentences, specific, friendly.
